Mission Brief

What I do, in plain terms

I'm an engineer who has spent a career in the parts of software that don't get to fail quietly: distributed systems, big-data platforms, and high-reliability infrastructure running at petabyte scale. That work has meant designing domain-specific tooling for querying enormous datasets, hardening platforms for reliability and runtime performance, and standing up high-performance compute clusters from nothing.

More recently, my attention has turned to applied AI — integrating models into production paths, evaluating where they genuinely help, and keeping the same reliability bar I'd apply to any other critical system. I'm most useful in roles where deep systems engineering and modern AI tooling need to meet without either one being treated as an afterthought.

  • Scale: Petabyte-class data, C# domain-specific tooling, HPC clusters
  • Reliability: Platform hardening for uptime, throughput, and runtime performance
  • AI: Applied model integration grounded in production engineering practice
